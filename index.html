How to Farm

Howdy, farmer! During the day, keep a sharp eye on your crops and watch where the shadows fall across the field. Consider the shadow of the fences unimportant.

When night rolls in, that is your time to farm. Pick your seeds and plant them on the tiles you think will give each flower the best chance to grow.

Each level intro will tell you what every flower needs. Flowers take one extra night to grow, and once daylight returns, you can harvest them for flowers and fresh seeds.

After harvesting a flower, let that patch of soil rest for one more day before planting there again.

Complete the level goals, move on to the next field, and show this farm what you are made of. Are you ready to farm?
